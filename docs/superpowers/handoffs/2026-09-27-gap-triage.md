## Method

* **Gap session / ratio**: the first stored session on or after the row's `ex_date`; ratio = adjusted close that session / the previous stored session. For mcap issue-size rows: the session between 40 days before and 5 days after the snapshot whose ratio is closest to the implied bonus factor; if none is within 6 %, the largest move is shown and the row is treated as share issuance.
* **Nearest action text**: bc purpose text (the non-open `price_adjustments` rows) or `corporate_actions` within +/-30 days of the gap session, preferring split / bonus / consolidation / reduction / scheme / demerger / rights wording. **Context**: the latest restructuring text (capital reduction, scheme, amalgamation, consolidation, merger, demerger) from 2 years before to 30 days after the gap.
* **Classification**, first rule that fires: (1) mcap bonus row without a matching price gap -> genuine (share issuance; suppress the row); (2) the same exact >30-day absence shared by >= 3 symbols, <= 120 days, no restructuring text -> genuine (exchange-wide surveillance / series move); (3) penny stock (price < 2 and a move of <= 20 paise) -> genuine tick move; (4) a >= x1.5 move that reverses within 5 sessions -> genuine one-off print (needs a data fix, not an override); (5) `-RE` rights entitlement -> genuine; (6) drop next to demerger text -> demerger; (7) drop on a rights ex-date (+/-3 days) that snaps to no clean ratio -> genuine; (8) drop within 6 % of a clean bonus / split ratio (1/1.25 ... 1/100, 0.4), at most x0.8 unless split/bonus text is near, halt <= 60 days (or text) -> likely split-or-bonus; (9) jump within 6 % of 2/4/5/10/20/25/50/100 with consolidation text, or after a <= 30-day halt without restructuring context -> likely consolidation; (10) halt > 30 days, restructuring context, or x>=3 / x<=0.3 with no clean ratio -> break; (11) otherwise genuine move (verify).
* NSE bands cap banded stocks at +/-20 % a day (ratio 0.8-1.2); a "genuine move" outside that on a banded stock carries a NOTE.
* Heuristic only: every proposed line needs a check against the NSE announcement. `break` is the safe default when unsure (history is excluded, nothing is rescaled); a wrong `factor` rescales history.
