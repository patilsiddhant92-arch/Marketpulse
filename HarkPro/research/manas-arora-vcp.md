## 1. Prior uptrend and stage
- **[P] S1 31:35–32:05:** He starts from a volume scan, then keeps only stocks "in an uptrend for last 3 months… higher high, higher low… see if the stock is up 30% in the last three months… Minimum 30%, more the better."
- **[P] S1 17:19–17:48:** "You have to see the last 3 months history… how much big volume was there… 5 million, 10 million, 20 million kind of volume in one day."
- **[P] S1 19:17:** A model setup "has gone really tight after going 100% up."
- **[S] S7/S8:** He wants a prior move of **at least 50%**. S8 puts it as "50% move in last 4–5 weeks" and S7 as "50% in 5–10 weeks", on heavy volume. He also wants "fast movers" with frequent days of ±5% or more (the "purple dot").
- **[S] S7, which says Manas gave this template in a YouTube interview; S8 gives the same list from his 2018 webinar:** close above ₹30; within 25% of the 52-week high; close > 50 SMA > 200 SMA; 200 SMA rising for at least 3 months; at least 50% above the 52-week low (S8 says the price has at least doubled); a new 52-week high within the last 4–6 months.
- **[P] S3 reply:** Stocks "resting on or around (±5%) [the] 10-period average on weekly frame and… within 25–30% of 52week High" are "your best bets".
- **[P] S6:** His scans are 52-week high, up 100% in one year, new 1-month high (plus an MA filter), and volume of 3× normal or more.
- **RS vs the index:** I found no numeric rule. **[S] S9** says he scans for "relative strength during corrections".

## 2. Base structure
- **[P] S1 43:50:** "What's the VCP in the book? A span of 4–5 months where the stock was down 40%, then 20%, then 10%… But for me volatility contraction is on every frame, on every bar."
- **[S] S8:** A good VCP "ideally has 3 contractions". Each contraction should be about 50% smaller in depth, and also about 50% shorter in time. If there are only 2 contractions, the second should be more than 70% smaller. The whole consolidation should last 4–8 weeks at most; "more than 8 weeks… avoidable".
- **[P] S4:** He calls a pattern a "2T VCP/CnH" when "the 2nd U shape is smaller in size by 70%".
- **[P] S3:** Cup-and-handle handles "deeper than 10% are loose & faulty usually". The handle "should ALWAYS form above the mid-point of the cup". One example he gives: "Cup under 25% deep, Handle under 10% deep & downward sloping."
- **Not found:** an exact depth sequence of his own like 25%→12%→6%.

## 3. Tightness
- **[P] S1 43:50:** "Yesterday bar was 4%, today is 1%. That's also VCP on a 2 days relative time frame."
- **[P] S1 19:17–19:41:** "Earlier [it had] 10% moves. Now its movement is confined to 1%… contracted to such low level… [take] a probability bet with a 2% stop."
- **[P] S5:** "If I have a tight zone in any up trending stock, I will take the breakout."
- **[S] S7:** The daily range before entry is "almost always less than 2–3%". Inside bars are common: daily inside bars under 2–3%, weekly inside bars under 3–4%.
- **[I]** He measures tightness bar by bar, as the high-to-low range in %, compared with the stock's own earlier range. He does not appear to use a fixed base-width formula.

## 4. Volume dry-up
- **[P] S1 36:43–37:39:** "Volume also has to reduce… If the volume is not coming down… it's something abnormal… Then it's a wrong setup… If it's low volume, take the trade. If it's high volume [in consolidation], stay away."
- **[S] S8:** Up days should come on high volume and down days on low volume.
- **Delivery %:** **[P] S5:** "I have been making money without ever checking the delivery volume before or after making the trade." So delivery % is not part of his method.

## 5. 10/20 EMA (and 50)
- **[S] S10:** He uses only price, volume and a 20-day MA.
- **[S] S7:** He uses the 21 EMA. Pullbacks stay above it "almost always"; stocks "contracting within 1–5% above 21ema are prime"; "a low piercing the 21 Ema is an even better set up but a close below is seldom tolerated."
- **[S] S10 contradicts S7:** it says he "prefers stocks that correct below the 20-day MA before resuming". **[I]** The likely common ground is an intraday undercut or a shakeout of the 20 MA that recovers. S2 calls this a "mini shake out".
- **[P] S2:** He trails with the 20 MA normally and switches to the 10 MA in weak markets.
- **[P] S5:** He trails the last portion "with 5 or 10ma whichever it is respecting the most".
- **[P] S4:** "Odds… much better when breakouts happen 'closer' to 50dma." He passed on a 2T VCP because it formed "at a huge distance away from the 50 DMA… EXTENDED."
- **Not found:** a numeric rule that price must hug the 10 or 20 EMA. The nearest is the S7 "1–5% above 21 EMA" observation, which is a summary and not his words.

## 6. Entry, stop and sizing
- **[P] S1 18:47 and 21:06:** He picks the stock on the daily chart and executes on the daily or 1-hour chart with a "strong start system": "volume [has come], gap up opening… or open and low same… no sellers at all."
- **[S] S9 (SVRO):**
  - **S**trong start: a gap-up that clears yesterday's high.
  - **V**: opens above the prior day's value area.
  - **R**VOL: 7–10% of the day's volume in the first 5–10 minutes.
  - **O**: buys the opening-range-break high.
- **[S] S7:** He enters in anticipation, within the first 1–2% of the move. He does not wait for a confirmed 4–5% breakout.
- **Stops:**
  - **[P] S1:** "1–2% stops". He started with 8% stops and worked down: "start with 8%, take 20 trades… come down to 6%…"
  - **[P] tweets:** "stop LOD" or "stop 2%".
  - **[S] S9:** below the day's low, "rarely wider than 2–3%".
- **Risk per trade:**
  - **[P] S1 45:17:** "on an average risk per trade is 0.2 to 0.5%" of the portfolio.
  - **[S] S9:** 0.25% initial risk and 10–30% allocation.
  - **[P] S5:** he does not want a maximum allocation below 25% in a stock. He pyramids into winners.
- **Exits:**
  - **[P] S5:** "Half between 3–5R & more at 7–10R", then trails the rest.
  - **[P] S2:** "sell when it is extended or when it hits your stop". He moves the stop to cost at about +7–8%.
  - **[P] S1:** average holding period is about 10 days. His win rate is "in 30s".

## 7. Disqualifiers
- **[P]** Extended far above the 50 DMA (S4).
- **[P]** Parabolic: "100% in 5 days, 10 days… it's dead… don't touch it for month or two or three" (S1 35:18–36:14). "Slow steady" at a 30–40° angle is fine.
- **[P]** High volume while the range contracts (S1).
- **[P]** C&H handle deeper than 10%, or below the cup midpoint (S3).
- **[P]** BSE-only stocks (S5). He mentions that ASM/GSM surveillance limits the universe (S1 22:04).
- **[P]** He re-enters 3–4 times. If it still fails, "it's usually a chop stock or going through distribution" (S1 38:35).
- **[S]** Base longer than 8 weeks (S8). A close below the 21 EMA (S7).
- **Earnings soon: not found.** I found no rule about upcoming results.

## 8. Market filter
- **[P] S1 02:06:** "If the market is below 10 or 20 moving average… stocks don't break out… the magnitude is 10%… number of setups is reduced."
- **[P] S2:** The index trading below its 20 MA is "not very strong condition for swing trading". In bad markets gains are "on an average… half" of normal.
- **[P] S1 24:00:** Account drawdown is his market gauge. Down 2% in a month means a week's break; another 1% down means a longer break. "Trades are not working for me. For me, that's a bad market."
- **Note:** In 2021 he wrote "I don't have any indices on my screen" (S5), so the index-vs-MA filter is a more recent stance.

## 9. His terms
- **SS / Strong Start:** a gap-up or open = low with early volume.
- **SVRO:** see section 6.
- **Mini shakeout:** price undercuts the prior low, reverses and crosses the high (S2).
- **Purple dot:** a day of ±5% or more on high volume.
- **Tight zone.**
- **Extended:** far from the 50 DMA, or from MAs generally.
- **"Dead" stock:** a stock that has gone parabolic.
- **2T/3T VCP:** the number of contractions.
- **No-follow-through sell:** a big down bar with no follow-up selling (S1 44:19).
- **Reversal play / falling knife:** a separate setup.
- **Not found as his terms:** low cheat, pocket pivot. "Tennis ball action" (S8) and "squat" (S7) are the summarisers' words.

## Proposed screener rules (NSE daily)
Status: **G** = hard gate, **R** = ranking factor. Basis: P = his words, S = summary, I = my inference.

| # | Rule | Threshold | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Liquidity / price | close ≥ ₹30; NSE listed; not in ASM/GSM; 50-day median turnover ≥ ₹5 cr [I] | G | S/P/I |
| 2 | Trend template | close > SMA50 > SMA200; SMA200 > SMA200 63 sessions ago; close ≥ 0.75 × 52W high; close ≥ 1.5 × 52W low; a 52W high in the last 126 sessions | G | S |
| 3 | 3-month momentum | close / close 63 sessions ago ≥ 1.30 | G | P |
| 4 | Prior thrust | max gain from the lowest low to the highest high within the last 25–50 sessions ≥ 50% | R (G for the strict variant) | S |
| 5 | Fast mover | count of days with \|ret\| ≥ 5% and vol ≥ 500k in the last 63 sessions ≥ 3 | R | S/P |
| 6 | Big-volume footprint | max daily vol in the last 63 sessions ≥ 3 × 50-day avg volume | R | P |
| 7 | Not parabolic | gain over the last 10 sessions < 60% and over the last 15 sessions < 100% | G | P |
| 8 | Not extended | (close − EMA50)/EMA50 ≤ 25% [I threshold] | G | P (concept) |
| 9 | Base length | sessions since the base high: 10–40 | G | S |
| 10 | Contractions | zigzag pullbacks (swing filter 3%); ≥ 2; each depth ≤ 0.6 × the previous one; if 2T, the last ≤ 0.3 × the first; first pullback ≤ 35% [I] | G (≥ 2, decreasing); R (ratio quality) | S/P |
| 11 | Final contraction | depth of the last pullback ≤ 10% (≤ 6% preferred) | G ≤ 10%; R lower is better | P/S/I |
| 12 | Tightness | today's range % ≤ 0.5 × 20-day avg range % **or** range % ≤ 3%; bonus for an inside day; bonus for a weekly inside bar < 4% | G (one of these); R (bonuses) | P/S |
| 13 | Volume dry-up | 5-day avg volume ≤ 0.7 × 50-day avg volume; the base's down-day volume < its up-day volume | G (first); R (second) | P/S |
| 14 | MA position | close ≥ EMA21 (EMA20); distance 0–5% ranks highest; a low below EMA20 with a close above it is a bonus | G (close ≥ EMA20); R (distance) | S/P |
| 15 | 10 EMA | close ≥ EMA10 [I], or a 10 EMA undercut and recovery | R | I |
| 16 | Pivot / stop | pivot = high of the last 1–5 sessions (the tight zone); stop = min(low of day, entry × 0.98); skip if (pivot − stop)/pivot > 3% | G (risk ≤ 3%) | P/S |
| 17 | Sizing output | qty = (equity × 0.25–0.5%) / (entry − stop), capped at 25–30% of equity | output | P/S |
| 18 | Market filter | Nifty 500 (or Smallcap 250) close > its 20 EMA. When it is below, cut size or show the list as watch-only | G (regime) | P |
| 19 | RS | 63-day stock return − 63-day Nifty 500 return > 0; rank by RS percentile; bonus if it held up during the index's last ≥ 5% drawdown | R | S/I |
| 20 | Delivery % | do not use it as a gate; at most a weak tie-breaker | — | P (he ignores it) |
| 21 | Earnings | no rule from him; optional exclusion if results fall within 3 sessions [I] | optional | I |

Suggested ranking score: weighted sum of rules 4, 5, 6, 10-ratio, 11, 12 bonuses, 13 dry-up depth, 14 distance and 19 RS percentile. Weighting tightness and dry-up highest is my inference, but it fits his "tight first, then strong start" logic.

His actual trigger is intraday: a strong start, open = low, and early RVOL. An end-of-day screener can only build the watchlist for the next day.
